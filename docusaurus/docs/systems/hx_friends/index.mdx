# HX Friends

HX Friends is a collection of robot hand designs built on the **[DYNAMIXEL](/docs/dxl/model_reference/)** ecosystem.

The [Community Showcase](/docs/common/ecosystem) shows how individuals, universities, and companies already use DYNAMIXEL actuators to build open-source robots — including many robotic hands. HX Friends continues that idea: explore diverse hand layouts on the same modular foundation, then invent your own.

## Why the DYNAMIXEL Ecosystem

| Strength | What it means for hand builders |
| :------- | :------------------------------ |
| **Versatile actuators** | One actuator family scales from a simple finger or gripper to high-DOF anthropomorphic hands and full robot platforms |
| **Open-source culture** | Labs and companies publish DYNAMIXEL-based designs and tools you can study, remix, and extend |
| **Proven for hands** | Peer projects such as MIDAS Hand, Orca Hand, Leap Hand, House of Dextra, OpenHand, and others already choose DYNAMIXEL for dexterous manipulation |
| **Modular building blocks** | Familiar protocols and replaceable joints keep designs maintainable as you iterate |
| **Global community** | A worldwide network of makers and researchers sharing projects proudly **Powered by DYNAMIXEL** |

Together, these strengths give you an **open, modular, and practical** path to invent the hand that fits your robot and your problem.

## HX Friends: HX5-D20

- **Type**: Hand
- **Features**: A **20 DOF** anthropomorphic reference with a thumb and four fingers — useful when you want to explore human-like coverage and fine multi-finger coordination.
- **Components**
  - Twenty [DYNAMIXEL XM335-T323-T](/docs/dxl/model_reference/x_series/xm_series/xm335-t323)
  - Five tactile sensors at the fingertips

## Get Inspired

Use these designs as references, not rules. Pose the joints, compare layouts, and notice how actuator choice and DOF change the feel of a hand.

Then invent something we have not shown: a new finger arrangement, a lighter gripper, a task-specific mechanism, or a hybrid that only your application needs. The best next HX Friend is the one you create.
