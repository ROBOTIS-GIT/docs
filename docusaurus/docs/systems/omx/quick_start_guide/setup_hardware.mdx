# Hardware Setup Guide

This guide covers the physical setup before you use OMX: fix the two arms to the table, plug them and the camera into the PC, and check that the PC sees them. Nothing moves in this guide.

Complete the [Assembly Guide](/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/assembly_guide) before continuing.

:::info
You need an assembled OMX with its camera, and a PC with Ubuntu.
:::

## 3. Check the Connection

Ask the PC what it can see. Open a terminal with **Ctrl + Alt + T**. Copy the box below with the button at its top right, paste it into the terminal with **Ctrl + Shift + V**, and press **Enter**.

```bash
ls -1 /dev/serial/by-id/ /dev/v4l/by-id/
```

You should see two `OpenRB-150` lines, one for each arm, and the camera's lines.

<TutorialMedia
  label="The terminal after the command. In the two lines that start with usb-ROBOTIS_OpenRB-150, the long number is outlined in red. In the two lines that start with usb-HD_USB_Camera, the camera's name is outlined in red."
  src="/img/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/setup_hardware/connect_omx_ls.webp"
  shape="free"
  width={702}
  height={276}
/>

The parts outlined in red are different on your PC. Each board has its own number, and another camera has another name.

<details className="omx-fix">
  <summary>Something different?</summary>

| You see | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Only one `OpenRB-150` line | Most often the follower's 12 V power is off. Check the adapter at the wall and at the follower. |
| No `OpenRB-150` line, or `No such file or directory` | Push both USB cables in fully, at the arm and at the PC. Use the cables from the OMX box. |
| No `Camera` line | Unplug the camera's USB cable and plug it in again. If that does not help, try another USB port. |
| A line is missing and its cable goes through a USB hub | Plug that cable straight into the PC. |

</details>

<details>
  <summary>How does it work?</summary>

  The leader takes its power from the USB cable. The follower's motors need more power, so the follower has its own 12 V adapter. The PC can see the follower only while that power is on.

  The PC talks to each arm through a small board named OpenRB-150. That is the name you see in the terminal.

</details>
