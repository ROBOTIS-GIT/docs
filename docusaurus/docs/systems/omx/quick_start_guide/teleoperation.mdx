# Teleoperation

Complete [Software Setup](/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/setup_software) before continuing.

You move the leader arm with your hand, and the follower arm copies every move. This is called **teleoperation**.

  label="A hand holds the leader arm by its two rings and moves it down, closes the gripper and lifts. The follower arm copies each move."

:::warning
- The follower moves by itself when the software starts. Keep its reach clear.
- To stop at any moment: **Ctrl + C** in Terminal 2, or pull the follower's 12 V plug.
:::

## 1. Start the Arms

If the arms are still running from [Bringup](/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/bringup), go on to the next section. If not, start them now with two terminals.

Open a terminal with **Ctrl + Alt + T**, and enter the robot container. This is Terminal 1.

```bash
cd $HOME/open_manipulator/docker
./container.sh enter
```

Then start [Zenoh](/docs/systems/omx/specifications/software#middleware). Leave Terminal 1 open.

```bash
zenohd
```

Open a second terminal with **Ctrl + Alt + T**. This is Terminal 2. Enter the robot container there too.

```bash
cd $HOME/open_manipulator/docker
./container.sh enter
```

:::danger[Fold the leader first]
Before you paste the next line, **fold the leader into the same shape as in the clip, and leave it folded.** At the end, the follower goes at once to the shape the leader is in.
:::

:::warning
After you paste the next line, the follower moves by itself to its start pose for about 10 seconds. Keep its reach clear.
:::

## 2. Move the Leader Arm

Move the leader slowly. Squeeze the finger loop on its handle to close the gripper.

<TutorialMedia
  label="A hand moves the leader arm, and the follower arm copies each move"
  src="/img/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/operation_guide/ai_teleop.gif"
  width={560}
/>

Check that:

- The follower copies the leader
- The gripper closes when you squeeze the finger loop

<details className="omx-fix">
  <summary>Something different?</summary>

| You see | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| The follower reached its start pose but does not copy | Press **Ctrl + C** in Terminal 2. Check the leader's USB cable. Then do [Set the Ports](/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/setup_software#4-set-the-ports) again. |

</details>

<details>
  <summary>How does it work?</summary>

  The leader's arm motors are switched off, so your hand can move them. They only measure their angles, 100 times a second. The follower's motors go to the same angles right away. That is why the follower copies you, and why it jumps if the two arms start in different shapes.

</details>

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard>

## 3. Stop Teleoperation

:::warning
Lower the follower's gripper onto the table first. The follower goes limp when the software stops.
:::

Press **Ctrl + C** in Terminal 2, then in Terminal 1. Lines with `ERROR` at the very end are normal.
