# Inference

Take your hands off. The AI model moves the follower now.

Before starting, complete [Training](/docs/systems/omx/imitation_learning/model_training).

You use the four terminals of [4. Bringup OMX and Start Cyclo Intelligence](/docs/systems/omx/imitation_learning/setup#4-bringup-omx-and-start-cyclo-intelligence) again. This time Terminal 2 starts only the follower.

![The four terminals placed two by two on the screen. Terminal 1, robot container: Zenoh, with zenohd. - The follower moves by itself, and it can move in ways you do not expect. Keep its reach clear.
- To stop: click **Stop** on the page, press **Ctrl + C** in Terminal 2, or pull the follower's 12 V plug.
:::

## 1. Start Everything, Without the Leader

Use Terminals 1 to 4, still inside their containers. If you closed one, open it again and enter its container as in [Bringup OMX and Start Cyclo Intelligence](/docs/systems/omx/imitation_learning/setup#4-bringup-omx-and-start-cyclo-intelligence). Then paste one line in each, in this order.

In Terminal 1, start [Zenoh](/docs/systems/omx/specifications/software#middleware):

```bash
zenohd
```

In Terminal 2:

```bash
ros2 launch open_manipulator_bringup omx_f_follower_ai.launch.py
```

In Terminal 3:

```bash
ros2 launch open_manipulator_bringup camera_usb_cam.launch.py
```

In Terminal 4:

```bash
cyclo_intelligence
```

### Stop

:::warning
Lower the follower's gripper onto the table first. The follower goes limp when its program stops.
:::

Click **Stop** on the page. **Stop** pauses the model and keeps it loaded. **Clear** also unloads it, for example before you load another model. Then press **Ctrl + C** in Terminals 4, 3, 2 and 1.

<details>
  <summary>How does it work?</summary>

  Many times a second, the model looks at the camera picture and the arm's angles, and plans the next few seconds of moves. With **3D Sim Deploy**, the plan only shows in the 3D picture. With **Real Robot Deploy**, it goes to the motors.

  The model has no "done" signal. It keeps going until you click **Stop**.

</details>

## Improve

This section is for after your first run, if you want OMX to do the task better.

How well imitation learning works depends on many things: what the camera sees, the light and the things around the robot, and how many tries you recorded and how alike they are. A second camera can also help. The camera on the wrist sees only what is in front of the gripper, and LeRobot, which trains the model in this guide, suggests two camera views.

### What the Camera Should See

OMX decides each move from the camera picture. So when it runs, the picture should look like it did when you recorded. You should be able to do the task by only looking at the picture. With one camera on the wrist, the picture can show no box at the start: the box should come into it as the gripper moves near.

### Other Usual Reasons

These are the usual reasons, not all of them. A model can also miss for other reasons, such as how many tries you recorded or how it was trained. They come from the [LeRobot guide](https://huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/en/il_robots#tips-for-gathering-data) and its list of [what makes a good dataset](https://huggingface.co/blog/lerobot-datasets#what-makes-a-good-dataset).

| What went wrong | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The scene changed after you recorded | Run OMX in the same place, with the same things around it, as when you recorded. |
| Your tries were not alike | Start every try from the same pose, and pick up the same box the same way. Discard odd tries. |
| The boxes were in too many places | Keep them in the same places at first. Add other places only after it works well, with about 10 tries at each. |
| The camera shakes | Fix the camera firmly. |

Fix what went wrong. If it was your tries, record them again. Then do [Training](/docs/systems/omx/imitation_learning/model_training) and this page again.

Still missing? Record more tries, or ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/robotis).
