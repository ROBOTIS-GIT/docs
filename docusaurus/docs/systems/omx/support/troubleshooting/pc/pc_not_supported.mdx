# OMX Does Not Start on a Virtual Machine, Windows, or Mac

The OpenRB-150 boards do not show up in `/dev/serial/by-id`, or the arms do not start, on a PC like these:

- A virtual machine, such as VMware or VirtualBox
- Windows, also with WSL
- macOS
- The first Jetson Nano (not a Jetson Orin)

The OMX software is made for Ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04, installed on the PC itself.

<SectionCard>

## 1. Use Ubuntu on the PC itself

Install Ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04 on the PC. It can be next to Windows (dual boot). Then follow [Move OMX](/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/move_omx) from the start.

To train AI models, the PC also needs an NVIDIA GPU. See [Which PC Can I Use?](/docs/systems/omx/support/troubleshooting/pc/which_pc)

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard>

## 2. On a Jetson, use a Jetson Orin

Use a Jetson Orin Nano or better. Jetson Orin NX and AGX Orin run more stably. Train the AI models on a PC with an NVIDIA GPU.

</SectionCard>
