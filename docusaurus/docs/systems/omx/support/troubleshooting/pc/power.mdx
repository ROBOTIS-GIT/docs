# What Power Do the Arms Need?

The follower needs a 12 V, 5 A adapter. Its plug is 5.5 mm outside and 2.5 mm inside. In some regions, the adapter is not in the box.

The leader needs no adapter. It runs on its USB-C cable.

If the follower has no 12 V power, its board may not show up on the PC: in [Connect OMX](/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/move_omx#connect-omx), you then see only one `OpenRB-150` line.
