# Do I Set the Motor IDs Myself?

No. Each motor gets its ID before shipping. Put each motor on the joint of its ID, as in [Assemble OMX](/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/assembly_guide).

<SectionCard>

## The motor IDs

The IDs count from the base to the gripper:

| Joint | Leader | Follower |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (base) | 1 | 11 |
| 2 | 2 | 12 |
| 3 | 3 | 13 |
| 4 | 4 | 14 |
| 5 | 5 | 15 |
| 6 (gripper) | 6 | 16 |

The motor model of each joint is in [Hardware Specifications](/docs/systems/omx/specifications/hardware).

</SectionCard>

A new motor, for example one that replaces a broken motor, needs the ID of its joint: see [How Do I Replace a Motor?](/docs/systems/omx/support/troubleshooting/motors/replace_motor)
