# Can I Use Another Camera?

Yes. Other USB cameras work too.

Fix the camera firmly, so it does not shake. Then check its picture as in [Check what OMX sees](/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/train_and_run_ai#check-what-omx-sees).

The camera frame in the OMX box is made for the camera in [Assemble OMX](/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/assembly_guide), under **Horn orientation, camera and finishing**. Another camera may need another way to fix it to the arm.
