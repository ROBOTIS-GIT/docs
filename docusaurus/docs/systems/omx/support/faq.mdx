# Frequently Asked Questions

Questions that OMX users often asked on the ROBOTIS Discord server, with the answers of ROBOTIS. If your question is not here, ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/robotis).

<SectionCard>

## Which PC Can I Use? \{#which-pc}

A PC with Ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04, installed on the PC itself. It can be next to Windows (dual boot). On a virtual machine, Windows or macOS, OMX may not be found or may not start: see [OMX Does Not Start on a Virtual Machine, Windows, or Mac](/docs/systems/omx/support/troubleshooting/pc/pc_not_supported).

### To train AI models

The PC also needs an NVIDIA GPU. ROBOTIS suggests:

| | Minimum | Recommended |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CPU | Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 | Intel Core i7 or AMD Ryzen 7 |
| Memory (RAM) | 16 GB | 32 GB |
| GPU | NVIDIA GPU with CUDA | NVIDIA RTX GPU |
| GPU memory | 6 GB | 8 GB |
| Storage | SSD with 100 GB free | SSD with 100 GB free |

### On a Jetson

Use a Jetson Orin Nano or better. Jetson Orin NX and AGX Orin run more stably. The first Jetson Nano is not recommended. Training runs better on a PC with an NVIDIA GPU.

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard>

## What Power Do the Arms Need? \{#power}

The follower needs a 12 V, 5 A adapter. Its plug is 5.5 mm outside and 2.5 mm inside. In some regions, the adapter is not in the box.

The leader needs no adapter. It runs on its USB-C cable.

If the follower has no 12 V power, its board may not show up on the PC: in [Connect OMX](/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/move_omx#connect-omx), you then see only one `OpenRB-150` line.

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard>

## Do I Set the Motor IDs Myself? \{#motor-ids}

No. Each motor gets its ID before shipping. Put each motor on the joint of its ID, as in [Hardware Setup](/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/assembly_guide).

### The motor IDs

The IDs count from the base to the gripper:

| Joint | Leader | Follower |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (base) | 1 | 11 |
| 2 | 2 | 12 |
| 3 | 3 | 13 |
| 4 | 4 | 14 |
| 5 | 5 | 15 |
| 6 (gripper) | 6 | 16 |

The motor model of each joint is in [Hardware Specification](/docs/systems/omx/specifications/hardware).

A new motor, for example one that replaces a broken motor, needs the ID of its joint: see [How Do I Replace a Motor?](/docs/systems/omx/support/troubleshooting/motors/replace_motor)

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard>

## The Leader Does Not Go to Its Start Pose \{#leader-start-pose}

That is normal. The leader never moves by itself: its arm joints have no torque, so it stays where your hand puts it.

When the arms start, the follower first moves to its start pose by itself. Then it takes the shape of the leader, and copies it. See [Start the arms](/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/move_omx#start-the-arms).

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard>

## Does the Leader Have Gravity Compensation? \{#gravity-compensation}

No. The leader's arm joints have no torque, so they move freely by hand.

The open_manipulator software has a gravity compensation controller (`om_gravity_compensation_controller`), which the OMY leader arms use. For the OMX leader it is turned off. To try it, start from the OMY code in [open_manipulator](https://github.com/ROBOTIS-GIT/open_manipulator).

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard>

## Can I Use Another Camera? \{#other-camera}

Yes. Other USB cameras work too.

Fix the camera firmly, so it does not shake. Then check its picture as in [Start OMX and Cyclo Intelligence](/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/train_and_run_ai#start-omx-and-cyclo-intelligence).

The camera frame in the OMX box is made for the camera in [Hardware Setup](/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/assembly_guide), under **Horn orientation, camera and finishing**. Another camera may need another way to fix it to the arm.

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard>

## Can the Leader and the Follower Be on Two PCs? \{#two-pcs}

Yes. Connect both PCs to the same network, and use the same `ROS_DOMAIN_ID` on both. If the ROS 2 topics of one PC reach the other, the leader on one PC moves the follower on the other. For example, the follower can run on a Raspberry Pi.

The Quick Start Guide uses one PC. How the programs talk to each other is in [Middleware](/docs/systems/omx/specifications/software#middleware), in Software Architecture.

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard>

## Can I Use Two OMX Sets Together, or One Leader for Two Followers? \{#two-sets}

Not with the official software.

ROBOTIS answered that it can be built with ROS 2: for example, to run two sets on one PC, to move two followers with one leader, or to use two arms together for one task.

- Run each arm in its own ROS 2 namespace.
- Control the arms from one node above them.

You write that part yourself: there is no official example.

</SectionCard>

<SectionCard>

## Where Do I Get a Repair? \{#repair}

Request a repair on the ROBOTIS A/S page. Then send the product to the address it shows, and ROBOTIS checks it.

Many motor problems can be fixed without a repair. Check these first:

- [The Follower Jumps or Does Not Match the Leader](/docs/systems/omx/support/troubleshooting/motors/arm_moves_wrong)
- [A Motor Blinks Red or Is Not Found](/docs/systems/omx/support/troubleshooting/motors/motor_not_found)

To request repair and service, go to [ROBOTIS A/S Request](https://www.robotis.com/shop/rma.php).

</SectionCard>
