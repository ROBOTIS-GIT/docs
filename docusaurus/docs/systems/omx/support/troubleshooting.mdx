### Move OMX

<FixCards>
<FixCard title="Install the software" to="/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/move_omx#install-the-software-fix">

- `'compose' is not a docker command`
- `permission denied`
- `'open_manipulator' already exists`

</FixCard>
<FixCard title="Connect OMX" to="/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/move_omx#connect-omx-fix">

- Only one `OpenRB-150` line
- No `OpenRB-150` line, or `No such file or directory`
- No `Camera` line
- A line is missing and its cable goes through a USB hub

</FixCard>
<FixCard title="Set the ports" to="/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/move_omx#set-the-ports-fix">

- `No such container` or `is not running`
- `./container.sh: No such file or directory`
- `No such file or directory` after `ls`
- Two `OpenRB-150` names after `ls`

</FixCard>
<FixCard title="Start the arms" to="/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/move_omx#start-the-arms-fix">

- `Unable to connect to a Zenoh router`
- `Error opening serial port!`
- `There is no status packet!`

</FixCard>
<FixCard title="Move the leader arm" to="/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/move_omx#move-the-leader-arm-fix">

- The follower reached its start pose but does not copy

</FixCard>
</FixCards>

### Train and run AI

<FixCards>
<FixCard title="Install Cyclo Intelligence" to="/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/train_and_run_ai#install-cyclo-intelligence-fix">

- `curl: command not found`
- `Refusing to modify an existing Cyclo checkout`
- `no space left on device` while installing
- No line after `grep ZENOH_CONFIG_OVERRIDE`

</FixCard>
<FixCard title="Check what OMX sees" to="/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/train_and_run_ai#check-what-omx-sees-fix">

- No camera picture, and `Device specified is not available`
- The picture of another camera, such as a laptop's own
- A red dot at `/joint_states` or `/leader/joint_trajectory`
- The Cyclo Intelligence page does not open

</FixCard>
<FixCard title="Record your tries" to="/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/train_and_run_ai#record-your-tries-fix">

- **Record Start** stays grey
- A red dot in **Topic Monitor**
- The number after **EP** does not go up
- No **EP** number on the page

</FixCard>
<FixCard title="Train the AI model" to="/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/train_and_run_ai#train-the-ai-model-fix">

- `NVIDIA-SMI has failed`
- `permission denied`, or no `Runtimes` line
- `No such file or directory` with `lerobot_v30`
- `already exists and resume is False`
- `CUDA out of memory`
- `lerobot_server is not running`
- `no space left on device` while building

</FixCard>
<FixCard title="Run OMX on its own" to="/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/train_and_run_ai#run-omx-on-its-own-fix">

- **Start** stays grey
- `Image missing`, or **ON** does nothing
- An error about the policy path

</FixCard>
<FixCard title="Going further: Make it work better" to="/docs/systems/omx/quick_start_guide/train_and_run_ai#make-it-work-better">

- The arm shakes, or misses the box most of the time

</FixCard>
</FixCards>
