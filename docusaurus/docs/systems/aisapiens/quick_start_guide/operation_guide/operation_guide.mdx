# Operation Guide

This guide covers basic hardware setup and power-on procedures for AI Sapiens.

## How to Turn On and Off the Remote Controller

Use the power button in the center of the RadioMaster Pocket, below the screen.

### Turn On the Remote Controller

1. Press and hold the power button.
2. When the controller vibrates, release the button.
3. Confirm that the screen turns on and the `ROBOTIS` logo appears.

1. Press and hold the power button.
2. Keep holding until the controller powers off and the screen turns off.

## How to Use the Physical Emergency Stop

AI Sapiens includes a physical emergency stop button attached to the robot. Use it to cut actuator power immediately when you need to stop the robot for safety.

For the remote controller E-STOP switches, see [E-STOP Switch Guide](/docs/systems/aisapiens/quick_start_guide/using_remote_controller/#e-stop-switch-guide).

### Press the Emergency Stop

Press the red emergency stop button firmly until it latches.

<video className="ai-sapiens-demo-video" controls autoPlay muted loop playsInline preload="metadata">
  <source src="/videos/systems/aisapiens/ai_sapiens_emergency_press.webm" type="video/webm" />
</video>

When the button is pressed, the robot enters the emergency stop state and actuator power is cut.

### Release the Emergency Stop

Release the emergency stop only after confirming that the robot and the surrounding area are safe.

1. Twist the red emergency stop button **clockwise** in the direction of the `RESET` arrows.
2. Allow the button to pop back out to the released position.

  <source src="/videos/systems/aisapiens/ai_sapiens_emergency_release.webm" type="video/webm" />
</video>

After the button is released, continue with the normal power-on and mode procedures before operating the robot.

## How to Insert and Remove the Battery

The battery pack slides straight into the battery bay on the robot torso, next to the physical emergency stop button.

Keep the battery in the same orientation as shown in the video. Do not rotate or tilt it while inserting or removing it.

Power the robot off before removing the battery. Insert the battery only when you are ready to continue the power-on sequence.

### Insert the Battery

1. Match the battery orientation to the video: keep the recessed handles facing outward and do not rotate the pack.
2. Align the battery with the bay, then slide it straight in until it seats fully.
3. Confirm that the battery sits flush with the surrounding panel.

### Remove the Battery

1. Confirm that the robot is powered off.
2. Grip the recessed handles on the battery pack.
3. Pull the battery straight out. Do not twist or rotate it.

## How to Power On and Off

The robot power button is on the battery pack side panel, next to the LED indicators.

Confirm that the battery is inserted and the physical emergency stop is released before powering on.

### Power On

1. Press and hold the power button on the battery pack for `1` second.
2. Confirm that the LED indicators respond and the robot begins its power-on sequence.

### Power Off

2. Confirm that the robot powers off.
