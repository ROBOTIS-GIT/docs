# Prepare Motion

Prepare a training NPZ and deployment CSV for your robot and training environment. Follow the source and retargeting guides below, then continue with your learning method’s motion conversion and task setup instructions.

## 1. Generate and Retarget the Source

Use [Kimodo](/docs/systems/aisapiens/motion_generation_retargeting/kimodo/), [GEM-X](/docs/systems/aisapiens/motion_generation/gem-x/), or [FlowHMR](/docs/systems/aisapiens/motion_generation/flowhmr/) for human motion as appropriate, then retarget it to the intended K1 revision.

- [Soma-retargeter](/docs/systems/aisapiens/motion_generation_retargeting/soma_retargeter/) exports a headered K1 CSV used by the existing converter recipes.
- [UMR](/docs/systems/aisapiens/motion_generation_retargeting/umr/) exports `_umr_qpos.npz` for UMR playback and `_umr.csv` for Cyclo conversion. The qpos NPZ is not a Cyclo training archive.

Retain the source motion, original frame rate, model revision, and conversion settings. Review foot contact, scale, facing direction, joint limits, speed, and loop continuity. Source motion and body-model licenses still apply.

## 2. Create the Platform Reference

The guides below include Soma-retargeter CSV and UMR CSV conversion commands. Run the converter inside the selected environment’s Docker container:

| Environment | Conversion and task setup | Motion directory |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cyclo Lab | [BeyondMimic](./cyclo_lab/mimic#how-to-add-your-own-mimic-task), [SuperMimic / BlinkMimic](./cyclo_lab/supermimic#how-to-add-your-own-task) | `source/cyclo_lab/data/motions/K1_rev2/<motion>/` |
| Cyclo MJLab | [BeyondMimic](./cyclo_mjlab/mimic#how-to-add-your-own-mimic-task), [SuperMimic / BlinkMimic](./cyclo_mjlab/supermimic#how-to-add-your-own-task) | `source/assets/motions/K1_rev2/<motion>/` |

Keep the source CSV separate from the converted training NPZ and deployment CSV. Preview the converted motion before training or deployment.

:::warning[Environment-specific NPZ files]
Cyclo Lab and Cyclo MJLab NPZ files are not interchangeable. Use the NPZ generated for your training environment.
:::

## 3. Assign the Motion to a Task

Use a 50 Hz NPZ converted for the selected environment and robot revision. In Cyclo MJLab, BeyondMimic, SuperMimic, and BlinkMimic use the same converted motion format.

Continue with task configuration and registration in the guide selected above. If you already have the converted NPZ and CSV, skip its CSV conversion instructions.

## 4. Prepare Deployment Files

Use `policy.onnx` and `sim2real.yaml` from the same training run. For motion-tracking policies, also prepare the corresponding motion CSV and follow the method’s Play and Export instructions.
