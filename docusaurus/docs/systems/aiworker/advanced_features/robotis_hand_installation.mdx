# ROBOTIS Hand Installation

If your application requires a multi-functional grip, you can replace the `RH-P12-RN` gripper with a `ROBOTIS Hand`. Follow these steps to perform the exchange:

1. Remove the `RH-P12-RN` Gripper: Carefully unscrew the mounting bolts and disconnect the communication cable from the current gripper.

2. Reorient the Mounting Bracket: Unscrew the mounting bolts on the `FRP42-A110K` bracket and rotate it by 90°. Align the bracket and fasten the bolts into the highlighted holes as shown in the image above.

![hand_mount_bracket](/img/systems/aiworker/advanced_features/robotis_hand_installation/hand_mount_bracket.png)

3. Enable terminating resistor:

Unscrew and remove the back cover of the `HX5-D20` Hand.

<img src="/img/systems/aiworker/advanced_features/robotis_hand_installation/hand_back.png" width="350"/>

Move the slide switch to enable terminating resistor as shown below.

<img src="/img/systems/aiworker/advanced_features/robotis_hand_installation/hand_slide_switch.png" width="500"/>

Carefully reattach the back cover.

4. Connect the Communication Cable: Plug the `4-pin JST cable` into the port located on the `ROBOTIS Hand`. Ensure the connector is fully seated to prevent signal loss or intermittent connectivity.

<img src="/img/systems/aiworker/advanced_features/robotis_hand_installation/hand_cable_port.png" width="350"/>

5. Secure the `ROBOTIS Hand`: Align the hand with the rotated `FRP42-A110K` bracket and tighten the mounting screws.

<img src="/img/systems/aiworker/advanced_features/robotis_hand_installation/hand_attached.jpg" width="350"/>

The hardware installation is now complete.
