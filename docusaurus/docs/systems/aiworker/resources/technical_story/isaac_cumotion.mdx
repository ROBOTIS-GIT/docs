## 1. Overview

{/* TODO: Lead with cuMotion — what motion problem you solved on AI Worker, and why GPU planning matters. Briefly note NvBlox + CenterPose as supporting packages. */}

This post focuses on running **[Isaac ROS cuMotion](https://nvidia-isaac-ros.github.io/repositories_and_packages/isaac_ros_cumotion/index.html)** on the AI Worker for GPU-accelerated motion planning.

Around that planner we also use:

- **[Isaac ROS NvBlox](https://nvidia-isaac-ros.github.io/repositories_and_packages/isaac_ros_nvblox/index.html)** — 3D reconstruction / ESDF for the collision world
- **[Isaac ROS CenterPose](https://nvidia-isaac-ros.github.io/repositories_and_packages/isaac_ros_pose_estimation/isaac_ros_centerpose/index.html)** — 6DoF object pose for grasp / place targets
