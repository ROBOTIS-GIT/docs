# Teleoperation

Before starting, complete [Bringup](/docs/systems/aiworker/quick_start_guide/bringup).  
This guide explains how to set up and operate the AI Worker using teleoperation.

Select your **Leader** (A1, A2, or LG2) for setup and launch instructions. Button controls are shared across all three Leaders.

<SectionCard>

## 1. Connect the Leader

(*The term `Leader` refers to the control device in the AI WORKER system)

<Tabs groupId="aiworker-leader-type">
  <TabItem value="a1" label="A1">

<div className="teleop-connection">

![Back of the A1 Leader](/img/systems/aiworker/quick_start_guide/teleoperation/back_of_the_A1_leader.png)

1. `Power Cable`: Connect the power adapter to supply power to the leader controller.
2. `USB Cable`: Connect a USB cable from the leader controller to one of the USB ports on the back of the `Follower`.

</div>

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem value="a2" label="A2">

<div className="teleop-connection">

![Bottom of the A2 Leader](/img/systems/aiworker/quick_start_guide/teleoperation/bottom_of_the_A2_leader.png)

1. `Power Cable`: Connect the power adapter to supply power to the leader controller.
2. `USB Cable`: Connect a USB cable from the leader controller to one of the USB ports on the back of the `Follower`.

</div>

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem value="lg2" label="LG2">

<div className="teleop-connection">

![Back of the LG2 Leader](/img/systems/aiworker/quick_start_guide/teleoperation/back_of_the_LG2_leader.png)

1. `Power Cable`: Connect the power adapter to supply power to the U2D2 device.
2. `U2D2 ON/OFF`: This switch is located inside a small recessed hole. The device is turned on when the inner white-dotted button is pressed.
3. `USB Cable`: Connect a USB cable from the U2D2 to one of the USB ports on the back of the `Follower`.

</div>

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## 2. Wear the Leader

<Tabs groupId="aiworker-leader-type">
  <TabItem value="a1" label="A1">

![A1 wearing appearance](/img/systems/aiworker/quick_start_guide/teleoperation/a1_wearing_appearance.png)

1. First, pull the inner belt tightly and fasten it around the waist.
2. Then, pull the outer belt and attach it over the inner belt using the Velcro.

![Secure upper arm strap](/img/systems/aiworker/quick_start_guide/teleoperation/a1_upper_arm_strap.png)

3. Wrap the strap around your upper arm and fasten the magnetic closure to secure your arm to the Leader link.

![Adjust the link lengths](/img/systems/aiworker/quick_start_guide/teleoperation/a1_adjust_link_lengths.png)

4. Adjust the shoulder, upper arm, and forearm links to fit your body. Pull and hold the spring-loaded pin, slide the link to the desired length, then align the pin with a locking hole and release it.

:::tip[Releasing the Upper Arm Strap]
When taking off the Leader, grasp the pull cord on the magnetic closure and lift it upward to release the upper arm strap.

![Release arm strap](/img/systems/aiworker/quick_start_guide/teleoperation/a1_release_arm_strap.gif)
:::

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem value="a2" label="A2">

![A2 wearing appearance](/img/systems/aiworker/quick_start_guide/teleoperation/a2_wearing_appearance.png)

1. Adjust the tripod to a comfortable operating height.

![Secure elbow strap](/img/systems/aiworker/quick_start_guide/teleoperation/a2_elbow_strap.png)

2. Put on and fasten the elbow straps to secure your arms to the Leader.

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem value="lg2" label="LG2">

**First Leader Version**

![Leader's wearing appearance](/img/systems/aiworker/quick_start_guide/teleoperation/leader_wearing_appearance.png)

1. Put both arms through the `Leader`'s `shoulder straps`.
2. Fasten the chest belt buckle and `hip belt buckle`.
3. Adjust the length of the `shoulder straps`, `chest belt`, and `hip belt` so that the `Leader` is securely fixed on your back.

**Second Leader Version**
It can be worn more easily by users with different body types.

<img src="/img/systems/aiworker/quick_start_guide/teleoperation/new_fixed_belt.png" alt="Web UI" className="doc-img-40" />

1. First, pull the inner belt tightly and fasten it around the waist.
2. Then, pull the outer belt and attach it over the inner belt using the Velcro.

:::warning[Check the Leader Joint Alignment Before Operation]
Before starting teleoperation, verify that the alignment stickers on every Leader joint line up as shown below. Do not start teleoperation if any stickers are misaligned, as this can cause unexpected or abnormal movement of the Follower.

## 3. Bringup the Leader

Complete [Robot Bringup](/docs/systems/aiworker/quick_start_guide/bringup) before starting the Leader.

1. In the **Leader** control box, select your Leader model: **A1**, **A2**, or **LG2**.

   <img src="/img/systems/aiworker/quick_start_guide/teleoperation/leader_type_selection.png" alt="Leader model selection in Cyclo Manager" width="386" style={{maxWidth: '100%', height: 'auto'}} />

2. Click the **Bringup (▶)** button to bring up the Leader.
3. Check that the Leader status light turns green. To view its logs, click the paper icon on the right.

   ![Leader service running in Cyclo Manager](/img/systems/aiworker/quick_start_guide/teleoperation/leader_bringup_button.png)

Leader bringup does not start arm teleoperation. Continue to the [Operation Guide](#4-operation-guide) to start operating the robot.

</SectionCard>

## 4. Operation Guide

<LeaderOperation />

### Stop Teleoperation

To stop teleoperation:
1. Return to a neutral position or pause the system.
2. In Cyclo Manager, click the stop button in the **Leader** control box.
