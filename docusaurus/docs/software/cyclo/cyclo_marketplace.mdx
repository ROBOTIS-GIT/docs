**Cyclo Marketplace** is a platform for sharing and selling **datasets**, **actions**, and **AI models**.

It brings these resources together so users can share their work or offer it for sale. Alongside [Cyclo Studio](./cyclo_studio.mdx), which creates actions through simulation and training and deploys them to physical robots, Cyclo Marketplace provides a place to distribute datasets, actions, and AI models.
