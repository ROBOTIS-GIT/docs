Controls the RGB LED on DYNAMIXEL PRO.

| Address | Color | Range |
| :-----: | :---: | :---: |
|563|Red|0 ~ 255|
|564|Green|0 ~ 255|
|565|Blue|0 ~ 255|


:::note

**NOTE** : The LED indicates present status of the device.

| Status  | LED Representation |
|:-------:|:------------------:|
| Booting | LED flickers once  |

:::
